# Contributing to Taste Index

Additions are welcome. The bar is **taste, not volume**.

## Adding an entry

1. Put it in the right table (galleries, niche, components or datasets).
2. Fill every column. Take sizes and pricing from the site itself and state them approximately (`~`, `+`).
3. Give the taste-filter rating honestly:
   - ★★★: hand-picked and selective; most entries are excellent
   - ★★☆: curated, but quality varies
   - ★☆☆: mostly volume or user-submitted
4. Datasets must list their license.
5. One entry per PR, with a short note on why it belongs.

## What doesn't belong

- Your own product's landing page (submit it to the galleries instead)
- Dead links, template marketplaces with no curation, link farms
